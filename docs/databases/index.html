Connected Literary Sinitic databases and local text imports

Classic Pattern Explorer v3.3 connects 19 online historical and Buddhist text sources across five regions alongside offline full-text searches on your own UTF-8 folders and imported bundles.

Note: Classic Pattern Explorer v3.3 connects 19 online sources, including Digital Engishiki (official browser search, CC BY 4.0) and the 7-database SHIPS selector.

Korea (8 online sources)

Classical collections, chronicles, archival manuscripts, and wooden-tablet transcriptions.

Korean Classics DB (ITKC)

Collections GO_AA · MO_AA · KP_AA

Searches ITKC classical originals (고전원문, 한국문집총간, 한국고전총간), returning excerpts of about 150 characters around hits within the first 100 records per collection. Translation collection IT_AA is excluded; nothing is stored automatically.

Cyber Seodang (Dongyang Classics DB)

Up to 50 records

Queries the Institute of Traditional Culture Open API and shows returned titles, descriptions, and article links. Full body text and original-vs-translation layer status are not guaranteed.

Korean History Database integrated search

First 20 rows · literal term

Queries NIKH public integrated search by era and database item with one continuous unquoted term, reading only the first original-text page (up to 20 candidate excerpts and /id/ links).

Annals of the Joseon Dynasty, NIKH search

First 50 results · literal term

Sends one continuous word or phrase to the NIKH Sillok original-text tab and reads the first page (up to 50 truncated candidate excerpts with article links). No pagination or crawling.

NAHF Northeast Asian History Net search

21 collections · first 20 rows

Searches one selected collection at a time with a continuous term and reads up to 20 candidate rows from the first original-text page.

NRICH · Wooden-tablet transcriptions

Up to 600 cached rows · literal term

Loads up to 600 public wooden-tablet transcriptions (Hanja and Idu, preserving uncertainty markers) per session and matches continuous literal terms.

AKS Korean Historical Manuscript Archive

First 20 rows · CC BY-NC-ND 2.0 KR

Queries the archive's original-text group (anciTXT) for up to 20 manuscript rows with highlighted transcription excerpts, dates, and UCI links. Personal non-commercial use only.

Kyujanggak historical documents · originals

First 20 references · up to 600 chars

Searches Seoul National University Kyujanggak GMS documents with one keyword (no spaces, up to 80 chars), returning titles, document numbers, and available inline excerpts.

Japan (5 online sources)

Digitized-book OCR, Buddhist canon line hits, historical diaries, Engishiki, and poetry headings.

National Diet Library, Japan (NDL)

OCR excerpts · min 2 chars · first 50 books

Searches NDL Lab digitized-book OCR text (requires a phrase of 2+ characters). Excerpts lack punctuation and may contain OCR errors; verify the page image via the title link.

SAT Daizōkyō Text Database

First 30 line hits · literal term

Reads the first group of up to 30 Taishō page/line hits with keyword-in-context excerpts. Image-only glyphs appear as □. Overlaps CBETA Taishō text while adding line references.

University of Tokyo SHIPS · official search

7 databases · browser link only

Prepares an official browser-search link for one keyword without spaces across 7 databases (Old Japanese diaries w16, Dai Nihon Shiryō w61, premodern w10, Nara w08, Heian w09, Kamakura w11, Nanbokuchō w29). Fetches no rows or counts.

Digital Engishiki (National Museum of Japanese History) · official search

v3.3 addition · browser link only · CC BY 4.0

Prepares an official browser-search link for one keyword without spaces on the provider's page, which filters text client-side. CPE fetches no rows or hit counts.

CanD Chinese and Japanese poetry · references

First 20 headings · max 10 chars

Queries Hankeidou's CanD poetry collections with variant expansion OFF, displaying the first 20 bibliographic headings and edition overview links without poem bodies or Japanese readings.

China (1 online source)

Stone-inscription catalogue transcription lookup.

Zhejiang University Chinese Stone Inscription Database · transcription search

First 15 catalogue references

Searches the transcription field across seven default collections with one keyword without spaces. Displays titles, dynasties, dates, object types, and record links without excerpts.

Taiwan (4 online sources)

Buddhist canon API, DocuSky public databases, Taiwan archival texts, and Old Deeds.

CBETA Chinese Buddhist Canon

~40 chars per side · first 50 juan · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Queries the CBData API and checks remaining pattern conditions within ~40-character excerpts around hits (excluding interlinear notes). Links to CBETA Online juan pages.

DocuSky · 佛寺志靈應故事 & 29 reviewed profiles

50 records/page · manual paging up to 5 pages

Searches one public DocuSky database at a time (including 29 reviewed profiles such as 佛寺志靈應故事, 承政院日記, 朝鮮王朝實錄, and 大明一統志, plus unreviewed catalogue entries marked accordingly).

Academia Sinica ITH Taiwan Full-text Database

First 20 rows · literal term

Queries the Institute of Taiwan History public search window with one continuous term and reads up to 20 candidate titles, excerpts, and links.

Taiwan History Digital Library · Old Deeds

First 10 results · up to 600 chars

Searches THDL's Old Deeds collection with one keyword without spaces, returning up to 10 candidate references and official document links. Ming/Qing administrative archives are outside this connection.

Vietnam (1 online source)

Hán-Nôm digitized corpus OCR search.

Việt Điện Hán-Nôm OCR corpus

First 20 page hits · literal term

Queries Việt Điện's public OCR search with one word or phrase, showing up to 20 page snippets and viewer links. Machine OCR may contain errors and matches glyph variants; inspect the page image.

Local text folders & supported importers

Up to four independent folder slots (local–local4) search UTF-8 .txt, .md, and .texts.jsonl bundles completely offline.

  • Korea public data portal XML/ZIP: NIKH (e.g. 삼국사기, 삼국유사, 고려사, 조선왕조실록 태조~철종, 비변사등록), ITKC, AKS Jangseogak registers, and NAHF (15163258 15-collection bundle, 동문휘고), plus batch release folder import.
  • Chinese Text Project (CTP/CText): Try a single chapter without an API key where CTP permits; full chapter lists require your own CTP API key.
  • Selected partial importers: MPIWG rare local gazetteers (up to 50 source units per preview), CBETA P5 XML, NDL OCR pages, Project Gutenberg ebooks, and the curated Work Finder selector.
  • Bibliographical sidecars: Place a NAME.txt.manifest.toml file beside a text to record title, edition, era, year, and year_end.

Scope & attribution boundaries

Classic Pattern Explorer is an independent personal project (MIT License) with no institutional affiliation. No database corpus, modern translation, or raw API dump is bundled.

Online search covers only the bounded excerpts or catalogue references returned by each provider within its request limits. An empty result never proves absence in a historical corpus.
